Straight answers

Common questions

Do you charge for estimates?
Basic estimates are free. Complex diagnostics carry a posted diagnostic fee that's applied toward the repair if you approve the work.
Do you work on my make and model?
Almost certainly. We service domestic and import cars, trucks, and SUVs, including light-duty diesels. If it's something we don't handle, we'll tell you up front and point you to someone who does.
How long will my repair take?
Most maintenance and brake jobs are same-day. Larger jobs get a time estimate with your written quote, and we call if anything changes.
Is there really a military discount?
Yes — active duty, veterans, and first responders. Just mention it when you drop off. No coupon needed.
